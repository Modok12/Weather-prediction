How do I weigh it with a bathroom scale?
  1. Close the valve and take off the regulator.
  2. Weigh yourself. Note the number.
  3. Weigh yourself again while holding the cylinder.
  4. Second number minus first number = weight of the cylinder.

A kitchen or luggage scale works for small (3–6 kg) cylinders. Weigh the same way each time.
